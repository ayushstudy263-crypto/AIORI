### Known Issues and Criticisms

- **Client drift under non-IID data.** Karimireddy et al. prove that FedAvg suffers client drift when client data is heterogeneous, giving unstable and slow convergence, and show it persists even with full-batch gradients and full client participation [18]. FedCM's authors cite earlier empirical findings of significant FedAvg degradation on non-IID client data [19].
- **FedAvg in traffic settings.** Banik et al. state that FedAvg, FedProx and FedRep tend to perform best when client data is IID [13]. In an online PEMS-BAY evaluation, FL error grew with the number of local epochs and non-IID mitigation (FedProx, SCAFFOLD) gave limited gains under low-latency constraints [17].
- **Gap to centralised accuracy.** Federated traffic methods stay substantially below centralised models on most datasets; FedeRICo reduces MAE versus FedAvg by 18.8% on METR-LA, 10.2% on PEMS-BAY, 24.6% on PEMS-D3 and 10.6% on PEMS-D7 [15].
- **Evaluation leakage.** Random splits on time-ordered data let future information reach training [33][34]. M3FGM uses a chronological 70/20/10 split [14], while FedeRICo reports a 60:20:20 ratio without stating order in the retrieved text [15].
- **Comparability.** Split ratios, local epochs, batch sizes and round counts differ across papers [14][15][16], and communication cost is reported per client for model distribution only in at least one work [5].
